How it works

A clear path from camera to control.

A modular workflow designed for staged pilots and evaluation before large-scale deployment.

Collect camera feeds

Receive traffic video from selected roads or toll lanes.

02 / ANALYZE

Detect and track

03 / PREDICT

Estimate traffic

Summarize density and evaluate congestion patterns.

04 / RESPOND

Support decisions

Present signal insights, toll checks, and alerts on a dashboard.
